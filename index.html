Legal AI & Compliance

Czech Legal Corpus (e-Sbírka)

A pristine collection of current-text Czech and historical Czechoslovak legislation dating back to 1918. Extracted from official e-Sbírka open data and fully structured for LLMs.

  • Coverage: ~46,000 acts (Zákon, Vyhláška, etc.) down to §/Čl. provision level
  • Quality: Pre-deduplicated, current-text consolidated, dual-stage language tagged (CS/SK)
  • Licensing: 100% Public Domain / Copyright-Free (EU Open Data Directive)
  • Format: JSONL (includes character counts, dates, and amendment metadata)
Literary & Language

Czech Literary Corpus (Pleias Alignment)

Curated set of public domain Czech books derived from the Pleias initiative. Ideal for expanding general Czech vocabulary, grammar, and long-form prose understanding.

  • Coverage: Comprehensive historical prose catalog
  • Quality: Standardized typography, OCR clean-up
  • Licensing: Open / Public Domain
  • Format: JSONL / Hugging Face Compatible
